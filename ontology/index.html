Ontology

The kinds of things on this site, how they connect, and how the map draws them: one icon for each kind, three colours, and what every line on the map means.

Everything on this site is one of a small number of kinds: something I wrote, something I made or arranged, or an idea or a work by someone else that the writing connects to. Each kind has one icon, and it is the same wherever the kind appears: in lists, on Start here, in search, and as its node on the map.

Each kind is registered in one place, a folder of pages or a data file, and reaches every page from there. This page is the key to all of it. The counts are live and change as the site grows.

Kinds

Three colours on the map, one for each group. Every kind is drawn the same way, as a disc with its icon, and a node grows with its connections.

My writing

What I wrote, and the hubs that gather things together on the map.

  • Article39 Long-form writing: an argument, a build, or a review, usually in chapters.content/articles/
  • Review2 An article about one work from the reading log, with a rating. Reviews are articles in the Reviews category.extra.review
  • Note8 A paragraph or a page on one idea. A note may later grow into an article.content/notes/
  • Shelf4 A status in the reading log, such as Backlog or Read, drawn as a hub that gathers its works.status, data/reading.toml
  • Project group6 A tier of projects, such as Ventures or Labs, drawn as a hub that gathers its projects.tiers, content/projects/

What I made, and how it is arranged

The talks and projects I made, and the series and categories that give the writing its order.

  • Talk1 A conference talk as slides, with its event and venue.content/talks/
  • Project33 Software, a site, or a study I build, with its status and its links.data/projects/
  • Series1 A reading order across several articles, read part by part.taxonomies.series
  • Category1 A form of writing rather than a subject. Today there is one: Reviews.categories_descriptions

Relations

Every line on the map is one of these. Most of them come from a single field on a page or in a data file.

  • is about Pages, projects, and works each carry their topics. This is most of the map.
  • sits under A topic and its broader topics. Some of these bridge fields on purpose, such as Risk under Reliability.
  • links to Every link in the text of one page to another. Both pages show it, as Links to and Linked from.
  • is part of An article and the series it belongs to.
  • reviews A review and the work it reviews.
  • discusses A page that discusses a work without reviewing it.
  • is in A page and its category, a form of writing.
  • is on A work and its shelf in the reading log.
  • is in A project and its tier on the projects page.
  • is related to Projects that belong together, such as Hotaru and Terrarium.

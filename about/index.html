About

Raffael is an AI architect in Basel, working on enterprise AI and agent platforms, edge systems, and applied security.

Hi, I am Raffael. I live in Basel and work as an AI architect.

By day I am Enterprise Solution Architect for AI Platforms and Agentic AI at Helvetia Baloise Group. I help define a Group-wide AI platform: a common foundation for the secure, governed use of foundation models, agents, and AI-assisted engineering across market units with different technology strategies. Before that, I spent the first half of 2026 there as a platform engineer on secure web delivery and edge infrastructure, where a colleague and I built an LLM-assisted workflow that turns noisy ModSecurity alerts into review items an operator can act on.

Outside of work, I build automation-first platforms and edge systems at the intersection of security and AI, and a lot more besides: programming-language tooling, local-first apps, simulations, and games. For years my ideas lived in a notebook because there was never enough time to try them. AI moved the bottleneck from writing the code to deciding what is worth building, so now I ship ideas small and real and see which ones stick. What I write here is my own view, not my employer’s.

raskell=rascal×Haskell×Raffael(tanuki mascot included)

Tech that serves humans. Boring by default, precise when required.

I like hard problems. The kind where latency, reliability, risk, and people all collide. Applied security is just one arena of that. It is systems under pressure with adversarial inputs and questionable signal-to-noise.

Most of my ideas start with reading: books, papers, and long articles, many of them far from my field. I keep a reading log of what I am reading, what is next, and what I have read, and I review a book when I have something specific to say about it.

Projects

I run several early-stage ventures and flagship builds, alongside a steady stream of experiments. The main ones:

  • Stable

    Zentinel Security-first reverse proxy built to guard the free web. Rust on Cloudflare's Pingora, explicit configuration over hidden defaults, and WAF, auth and other policy in isolated agents, so a crashing agent leaves the proxy running. Agents for WAF, bot management, identity, API inspection and scripting, SDKs from Rust to Haskell, a pure Rust ModSecurity engine and a fleet control plane.

    Website
  • Open beta

    Archipelag.io Decentralized AI compute: AI jobs run on a network of community-operated GPU hosts. Jobs prefer nearby hosts and a preferred region, and every job records where it ran; strict regional enforcement is in development. In free open beta: hosts earn virtual credits while metering is validated, and real-money payouts are not enabled yet.

    Website
  • Public alpha

    Cyanea Bioinformatics notebooks that actually re-run. Write, run, and share analyses that execute in your browser on WebAssembly, with nothing to install. Every notebook, dataset, and output is a versioned snapshot you can run again. A Rust analysis stack underneath, 21 file formats built in. Open source, with self-hosting and federation on the way.

    Website
  • Shipping

    Arcanist Haskell tooling for the age of AI-authored code. hx is a fast package and project manager written in Rust, and bhc is a compiler built to check everything it can. With a registry that records what has been verified, they lead toward a commons where nobody, human or model, has to verify the same thing twice.

    Website

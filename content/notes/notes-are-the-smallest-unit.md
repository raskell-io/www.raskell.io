An article is where I work an idea through properly: with code, trade-offs, and the counterarguments. That takes weeks, and most ideas never get there. They sit in a private notes app, half-formed, and nobody sees how they connect to the rest.

So this site now has notes. A note is a paragraph or a page: an observation from work, a paper worth remembering, a question I have not answered. Notes are cheap to write and easy to link, and every link becomes a line on [the map](/map/). Some of them will grow into articles, and the map will show which ones did.

This is the first step of what I described in [This Site Is Becoming a Map](/articles/this-site-is-becoming-a-map/): smaller nodes next to the long articles, so the graph fills in between them.

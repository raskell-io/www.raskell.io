Agentic engineering has been one person and their agents. Krzysztof Cieślak of GitHub Next showed the other version at [Agentic Engineering Days Zurich](/articles/agentic-engineering-days-zurich-2026/): Ace, a multiplayer workspace where a team shares a chat, its agents and their microVMs, and [Chopin](https://githubnext.com/projects/chopin/), its successor, where coworkers and agents write a plan together around a repository.

I think this is where work goes. Not one person prompting one agent and pasting the result into a document, but a conversation with several people and several agents in it, where the decisions, the designs and the code are produced inside the conversation and stay attached to it. The artifact keeps the argument that made it.

Zed got there early from the editor side. [DeltaDB](https://zed.dev/blog/introducing-deltadb) records every edit between commits and ties each change to the agent conversation that produced it, on top of an editor that was multiplayer before agents were a topic. They came ahead of their time and lost some momentum; I am still rooting for them. [The agent factory](/notes/the-agent-factory/) is the execution side of the same picture; this is the deciding side.
